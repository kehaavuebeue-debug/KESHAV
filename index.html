Our Story

A family restaurant in the heart of Dehradun.

Dalchini is a family restaurant in central Dehradun, located on the 1st Floor of Hotel Doon's Pride on Gandhi Road — close to Prince Chowk and the Dehradun Railway Station. It's an easy, welcoming place for family dinners and casual outings.

Our kitchen brings together flavourful Indian cooking, familiar Chinese favourites and a wide range of vegetarian and non-vegetarian dishes — from tandoor and curries to noodles, fried rice and biryani. With comfortable seating, a private dining room and an average rating of 4.6/5 from 668 reviews, Dalchini is built around warm, unhurried family moments.
